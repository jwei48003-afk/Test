# London Trip Plan

## Overview

A four-day trip through London's highlights: world-class museums, royal landmarks, riverside walks, and lively neighbourhoods. Most major museums are free, and the city is easy to explore by Tube and on foot.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Bloomsbury & the British Museum
- **Morning:** British Museum (Rosetta Stone, Parthenon sculptures, Egyptian galleries). Entry is free; book a timed slot online.
- **Afternoon:** Stroll through Bloomsbury and Covent Garden.
- **Evening:** Dinner and a West End show in Soho.

### Day 2 – Tower of London & the City
- **Morning:** Tower of London — see the Crown Jewels and join a Yeoman Warder tour.
- **Afternoon:** Walk across Tower Bridge, then visit Borough Market.
- **Evening:** Sunset views from Sky Garden (free, book ahead).

### Day 3 – Westminster & Royal London
- **Morning:** Westminster Abbey, Houses of Parliament, and Big Ben.
- **Midday:** Walk through St James's Park to Buckingham Palace for the Changing of the Guard (check schedule).
- **Afternoon:** Cross Westminster Bridge to the South Bank and ride the London Eye.
- **Evening:** Riverside walk along the South Bank to Tate Modern.

### Day 4 – Camden & Notting Hill
- **Morning:** Camden Market and a canal walk along Regent's Canal.
- **Afternoon:** Notting Hill — Portobello Road Market (biggest on Saturdays) and colourful streets.
- **Evening:** Farewell dinner in Kensington or Marylebone.

## Getting Around

- **Contactless / Oyster:** Tap in and out with a contactless card, phone, or Oyster card. Daily fare caps apply automatically.
- **The Tube:** Fastest way across the city. Avoid rush hours (07:30–09:30, 17:00–19:00) when possible.
- **Buses:** Cheap, scenic, and contactless-only (no cash). Hop on a route like the 11 or 15 for sightseeing.
- **Walking:** Many central sights are closer than the Tube map suggests.
- **Apps:** Citymapper or TfL Go for routes and live updates.

## Food Recommendations

- **Full English breakfast** at a local café.
- **Fish and chips** — try Poppies (Spitalfields/Camden).
- **Borough Market** for street food and snacks.
- **Sunday roast** at a traditional pub.
- **Curry on Brick Lane** or at Dishoom.
- **Afternoon tea** — a classic treat, from budget to luxury.
